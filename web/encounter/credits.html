Artwork

The human and mechanical hands were generated for CFI with the image_gen tool, then refined to leave the central glass clear for actual contributions. The illustration supplies a setting. The words, participant positions and influence indicators are separate live interface elements.

Inside the instrument

The local participants use Ollama. Their requests, returned text and available generation probabilities are kept in the encounter receipt. Their allocations are declarations, not internal attention.

Under the Glass measures a separate SmolLM2-135M-Instruct model. Its weights stay unchanged. Each measurement records the exact input, file hashes, model revision and intervention.

The seed is a separate random-born byte transformer. An explicit training action fits a new copy to the chosen text; it does not automatically make that text a collective decision.

Sound

The optional modem ritual is synthesised with Web Audio oscillators. It is not a sampled recording of a real modem or a real telephone connection.

This is a local working proposal. Its artwork, controls and terminology remain open to revision.
