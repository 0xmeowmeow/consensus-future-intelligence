What the numbers mean

Helpful / unhelpful is feedback on a message’s contribution to the conversation. Support / opposition is the existing shared allocation: one absolute unit per identity across the encounter. Neither is a truth score or an attention measurement.

A marker is an attributed interpretation. Agreement requires explicit endorsements; silence is not counted.
