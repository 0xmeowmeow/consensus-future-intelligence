WHAT THE EXCHANGE DOES

The other voices
get a hand.

  1. Ask something. One to eight different local models take sequential turns. Each receives the exact earlier conversation and can reply or pass. You can add yours at any time.
  2. Inspect before endorsing. Every human and model has one share, divided across as many contributions as they choose. Positive supports those exact words; negative opposes them. Viewing and hovering spend nothing.
  3. Keep the exception. Qualify a sentence, object to it, or reframe the question. The relationship and your exact text are recorded together.
  4. Let the disagreement develop. Ask the guests to consider new contributions or follow a particular branch. Their previous allocations remain in the receipt.
  5. Leave a trace. Closing saves the whole encounter. It does not turn the most popular sentence into truth or train it automatically.

Model allocations are generated declarations, not internal probabilities. The token inspector shows actual generation probabilities when returned by Ollama. The seed is a separate, small transformer with inspectable attention and gradients; it is not the internals of the guest models.

This revision has its own local state. Your original pilot, tokens and GRU checkpoints remain at port 8771. No payments or donated compute run here.

Only contribute material you have the rights or permission to use. The trusted local circle can read its shared receipts.
