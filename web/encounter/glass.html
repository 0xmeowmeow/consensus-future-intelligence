These are measurements.

Every strip in the volume is one attention head looking backwards from the final input position. There are 30 layers and 9 heads per layer. Horizontal position follows the actual input tokens. The raised contour and brightness encode attention weight.

The right-hand vocabulary is the model's final next-token softmax. These are possibilities for the next token, not independent minds and not measures of endorsement. A token can be only part of a word.

Muting a head zeros that head's output at the final input position, before its output projection. The model is then run again on the identical input. The measured probability differences show the effect of this particular intervention. They do not establish a permanent word meaning for the head.

Clicking a next token computes the derivative of its negative log likelihood with respect to the selected layer's final-position activation vector. This is a real gradient of a stated objective. No training or weight update occurs.

This instrument uses SmolLM2-135M-Instruct, a small pretrained model. It does not expose the hidden states of the other participants. The exact model revision, formatted input, values and intervention are in the downloadable measurement.

Rotation changes your view of the data only. It does not steer the model. Input changes and interventions are explicit controls.
